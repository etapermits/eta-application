What is the UK ETA?

The UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) is a pre-travel permission required by the United Kingdom for visitors from eligible countries who do not need a visa. It is linked electronically to your passport and must be obtained before you travel.

The UK ETA does not guarantee entry — Border Force officers retain the right to refuse admission on arrival — but it is a mandatory requirement to board a flight or vessel to the UK.

It is valid for multiple trips over two years or until your passport expires, whichever comes first.

Who needs a UK ETA?

The UK ETA is required for nationals of many countries who previously could visit the UK without any prior authorisation. This includes visitors from the United States, Canada, Australia, the Gulf states, and many others.

Nationals who already require a UK visa are not eligible for the ETA — they must continue to apply for the appropriate visa. British and Irish citizens do not need an ETA.

Frequently asked questions

When did the UK ETA become mandatory?
The UK ETA was introduced in phases from 2024. It is now required for all eligible nationals before travelling to the United Kingdom, including for transit through UK airports.
Do I need a UK ETA if I am transiting through a UK airport?
In most cases yes — even if you are transiting through a UK airport and not leaving the international zone, you will require a UK ETA or appropriate visa. Some nationalities are exempt from transit requirements; check the full list at ETA Permits.
How long can I stay in the UK on an ETA?
Each visit to the United Kingdom under an ETA is limited to six months. The ETA itself remains valid for two years or until your passport expires, allowing multiple trips within that period.
Can I work or study in the UK on an ETA?
No. The UK ETA is for tourism, visiting family and friends, short business trips, and transit only. If you intend to work or study in the UK you will require a separate visa.
What happens if my UK ETA is refused?
If your ETA application is refused you will need to apply for a UK visa through the standard visa application process. ETA Permits reviews all applications before submission to minimise the risk of refusal.
Do I need to print my UK ETA?
No. The UK ETA is linked electronically to your passport. You do not need to carry a printed copy — simply present your passport at check-in and at the UK border.
